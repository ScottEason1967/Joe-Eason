Built for the middle of the park
Athlete first

Joe plays box-to-box in senior men's football, on top of nine years of independent strength & conditioning with PROformance. That athletic platform is what the modern central midfielder is built on: the engine to press and recover, the power to win duels, and the frame to hold his own in the middle of the park.

He is also genuinely quick — verified elite times over 5m, 10m and 30m, topping out at 34 km/h. Rare pace on a 6'2", 89.9kg frame, and it's what lets him recover ground after a turnover and still arrive in the box at the other end.
